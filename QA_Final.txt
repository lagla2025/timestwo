TIMES TWO — FINAL Q&A (LOCKED)

1. HOW DOES THIS ACTUALLY WORK?

Two parts: data and matching.

On the data side, this works through publisher partnerships.
Users connect their account using OAuth. Permission-based, fully opt-in, and revocable at any time.

On the matching side, we use existing AI APIs.
We pass behavioural signals in, generate the Wingman output, and return it to the user.

What we own is the system:
the data pipeline, the matching logic, the prompt layer, and the product.

2. WHAT STOPS HINGE OR BUMBLE FROM DOING THIS?

Their business depends on the current mechanism.

Swipe, engagement loops, time spent. That's the revenue engine.

They can optimise it, but they can't replace it without breaking their model.

Times Two starts from a different place:
inside a subscription ecosystem where the goal is connection, not engagement.

3. WHY WON'T PUBLISHERS BUILD THIS THEMSELVES?

They don't build new categories from scratch. They buy proof.

The Athletic. Wordle. Wirecutter.

We're building the proof first.

And even if one publisher built it, they wouldn't aggregate across competitors.

The value sits in how the matching works. Not just where it lives.

4. HAS THIS BEEN TRIED BEFORE?

Yes. Guardian Soulmates.

500,000 members.
15 million pounds in annual revenue.

It worked.

But it was profile-based, competing against free apps.

Times Two uses behavioural data instead.

Same audience. Different mechanism.

5. WHAT IF PUBLISHERS SAY NO?

We don't need all of them. We need one.

The Guardian is the natural starting point.

If one proves it works, others follow.

If not, we can launch independently using user-connected data and prove the model.

Phase 1 is designed to answer that before we scale.

6. WHAT ABOUT PRIVACY?

Everything is opt-in.

Users explicitly connect their account and choose what they share.
They can revoke it at any time.

And importantly, this flips the usual model.

Most platforms use your data to target ads.

We use it to introduce you to someone you'll actually want to talk to.

It's one of the few cases where your data is used directly for your benefit.

8. WHAT ARE THE RISKS?

There are three real risks.

First, publisher adoption.
That's why Phase 1 generates evidence using their own subscribers before we ask for a deal.

Second, cold start.
